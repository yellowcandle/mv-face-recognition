# Analytics Specification

## Overview
A dashboard for exploring contestant screen time statistics, co-appearance patterns, and trends over time. Users can filter by video, view leaderboards, and click through to contestants or videos.

## User Flows
- View screen time leaderboard as a sortable table
- See top co-appearing contestant pairs ranked by shared screen time
- Select contestants to compare their screen time trends in a line chart
- Filter all analytics by specific videos
- Click a contestant to open their modal (from Contestant Directory)
- Click a video to open it in the Video Player

## UI Requirements
- Sortable table with screen time, appearance count, avg per video
- Top pairs list showing contestant duos and shared time
- Line chart for comparing selected contestants over videos
- Video filter dropdown/selector
- Clickable contestant names/photos linking to modal
- Clickable video titles linking to Video Player
