# Contestant Directory Specification

## Overview
A browsable directory of all contestants displayed as photo cards in a grid. Users can search by name or nickname, and click any card to view an expanded modal with stats and appearance history.

## User Flows
- Browse contestant cards in a responsive grid layout
- Search contestants by Chinese name or nickname
- Click a card to open expanded modal
- View contestant stats (total screen time, appearance count) in modal
- View appearance history with video titles and timestamps
- Click an appearance to jump to that video at the specific timestamp

## UI Requirements
- Card grid with photo, Chinese name, nickname, age
- Search input for name/nickname filtering
- Modal overlay with larger photo and full details
- Stats section showing screen time and appearance count
- Appearance history list with video links
- Click-to-play links that navigate to Video Player section
